MPARIVAHAN ANANTNAG V11.37 — CALENDAR SELECTION COMPATIBILITY FIX
===================================================================
Base: V11.36 consolidated recovery and booking safety. Original ZIP unchanged.

Reported symptom from V11.34.1: Calendar recognized, notification fired, but
assisted booking did not select green dates and never reached the quota/OTP flow.

Root-cause candidates confirmed in V11.34.1 code:
1. Green date required A[href*="fetchdlslotinfo.do"] even though archived
   diagnostic shows green TD with a child A and pointer cursor but does NOT
   establish this href pattern. Therefore every date may have been discarded.
2. Month/year reader only accepted contiguous "Month YYYY" heading.
   Archived diagnostics do not establish this precise header format.
3. If there were zero eligible Wed/Thu candidates or automation disabled,
   the extension did not surface sufficiently precise reasons to the user.

Fixes:
- Green date discovery now uses visible #calview td.cal_green; accepts a visible
  child anchor regardless of its href, or a pointer-enabled green TD as backup.
- Reads the date number from TD (instead of an anchor that might have no text).
- Safely parses varied calendar month header formats and rejects ambiguity.
- Shows explicit reason when there are no eligible Wed/Thu dates, when calendar
  month/year cannot be established, or when assisted booking isn't enabled.
- Verifies the selected control is still attached before clicking, with the
  existing tab/session action-gate and persistent pending-date tracking.

Important limits:
- Wednesday/Thursday-only booking selection is unchanged.
- Numeric quota/radio association remains conservative: no guessing based on
  unrelated numbers. If the actual quota table does not match verified layout,
  manual review is required. BOOK SLOT is not guaranteed to reach OTP.
- No automatic OTP/CAPTCHA completion or bypass.
- No live Sarathi booking or Telegram integration testing was performed.
- Monitor 8-second retry, background recovery, single-tab ownership,
  booking lock, notifications, audio, and existing UI are preserved.
