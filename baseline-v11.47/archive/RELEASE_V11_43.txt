MPARIVAHAN ANANTNAG V11.43 QUOTA COMPATIBILITY AND REVIEW ALERT FIX
Base: our V11.41.0, continuing the V11.34.1 lineage.
V11.42 was described as a separate third-party build and was not provided for
inspection or used as source. Version 11.43 avoids confusing the two branches.

FOUR REQUESTED FIXES
1. Plain-text quota: a verified radio cell whose complete visible text is one
   nonnegative safe integer is accepted, with or without a span. Positive quota
   means >=1. Existing span and separate-column layouts remain supported.
   Text such as "13 seats", multiple numbers, fractions, negative/unsafe integers,
   mismatched COV mappings and disabled positive-quota radios remain rejected.
2. Unexpected booking authorization refusals now pause with an explanatory
   manual-review alert identifying QUOTA_SELECT, BOOK_SLOT or BOOK_SLOT_CONFIRM.
   User STOP, disabled assistance and session replacement remain quiet.
   Existing reservation cleanup releases a known no-click reservation; attempted
   clicks and uncertain results stay locked. Cancellation failure cannot authorize
   a duplicate click; the persisted lock remains until explicitly resolved.
3. Calendar-loop branches yield through a STOP-aware 500ms wait, including when
   another inspection is already in progress. This is not a new monitoring rate:
   the original 8-second minimum pre-Proceed wait remains unchanged.
4. Manual-review warnings are deduplicated separately by monitoring session,
   warning kind, normalized reason and tracked date. Each has its own 10-minute
   timestamp. Repeating reason A after reason B is still suppressed independently.
   Records expire and are bounded to the most recent 100 entries; START clears them.

LIMITS
Tests use mocked Chrome/network APIs and minimal DOM fixtures. No live Sarathi
booking, real Telegram delivery, full browser end-to-end run or actual Chrome109
sound playback was tested. We do not claim every live website behaviour works.
After all eligible dates have zero quota the existing manual-review pause remains.
Manual-review warnings do not newly start the repeating sound; existing alert
sound behaviour is unchanged. Historical reports/releases are retained as history.
